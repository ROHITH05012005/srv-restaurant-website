Sri Raghavendra Vaibhava is a premium vegetarian restaurant known for authentic taste, hygienic preparation, quality ingredients, and warm hospitality.

We proudly serve delicious breakfast, lunch, dinner, chats, snacks, juices, tea, and coffee in both AC and Non-AC dining spaces — designed for comfort and cherished family dining experiences.
